### Descripción

Bikee is an innovative project that aims to make renting bicycles more efficient and user-friendly. Our app allows users to easily rent electric and conventional bicycles at affordable prices.

Our challenge is to create a platform that gives users access to the location, price, and type of bicycle to rent. We also aim to facilitate payment and generate reports for administrators.

With Bikee, users can rent bicycles with ease and explore their cities in a sustainable way.
